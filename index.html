Can a beginner learn cybersecurity?

Many learners start with foundational networking, operating system, and security concepts. The best starting point depends on your current skills and goals. Book a free guidance session to discuss your options.

What is the difference between CEH and SOC Analyst training?

CEH focuses on ethical hacking concepts and security assessment. SOC Analyst training focuses on security monitoring, log analysis, alert triage, and incident response.

What is the CPENT course fee?

CPENT pricing is provided on enquiry. Contact the institute to confirm the offering, prerequisites, schedule, and what is included.

Are certification exam fees included?
